# Themes

A **theme** is a look, worn all at once: a wallpaper, a plank for the spines, a set of colours, light or dark, how the panels are shaded, and which [ornaments](ornaments.md) stand on the shelf.

There are three kinds:

**Custom theme**
:   Your own settings. Choosing another theme never changes it, so it's always there to come back to.

**Plain**
:   Built in: no wallpaper, the oak plank, the standard colours and no ornaments.

**Packs**
:   Every [theme pack](packs.md) you've installed. A pack brings its own wallpaper, plank, colours and ornaments. Anything it doesn't bring comes from your Custom theme.

## Choose a theme

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

Open KOReader's menu at the top of the screen. The Bookshelf tab has a row named after the theme on screen: **Theme (Custom theme)**, say. In it:

- **This shelf**: the theme for the shelf you're on.
- **Other shelves**: a row for each of your other shelves. Choosing one switches to that shelf, so you see the change.
- **Default theme**: what every shelf without its own theme wears.

Each opens the **Theme library**: a card for each theme, with icons for what it brings. **Tap a card** and the shelf behind changes. The library stays open so you can compare. **Close** when you've decided.

You can also choose a shelf's theme from its editor: long-press the shelf's tab, **Shelf style**, then **Theme**.

## Edit a theme

!shot:side theme-menu | The Theme menu, top menu > Bookshelf.

Everything else in the **Theme** menu edits the theme on screen, whichever it is:

| Row | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| **Light or dark** | **Auto (follow night mode)**, **Light** or **Dark**. A dark shelf while you read in light mode, or the other way round. |
| **Wallpaper** | The picture behind the shelf, and the panels over it. See [Wallpaper and colours](wallpaper-colours.md). |
| **Plank** | The plank spines stand on: **Oak**, **Plain color**, or one from a pack. |
| **Ornaments** | Which ornaments this theme stands on the shelf. See [Ornaments](ornaments.md). |
| **Colors** | Every colour on the shelf. |

Your changes stay with that theme, on every shelf that wears it, and its card says **Edited**. To undo them, mark the theme in the Theme library and tap **Reset**, or long-press a pack's card and choose **Reset to original**.

### Planks

**Theme > Plank** opens the plank browser, each plank drawn as the shelf draws it. **Oak** is built in. **Plain color** tints a plain plank any colour you like. Packs add their own, with a tab each.

The [Planks pack](packs.md#planks) adds nine timbers, from pale **Maple** to near-black **Ebony**.

## A theme per shelf

Any shelf can wear its own theme, and the others carry on with the default. A shelf inside a [shelf of shelves](../organise/shelves.md#shelves-inside-shelves) wears its parent's theme until you give it one.

!shot:side theme-light-dark | Theme > Light or dark.

!!! tip "Night mode"
    Themes have light and dark colours. **Light or dark: Auto** follows KOReader's night mode. Pin a theme to **Dark** and it stays dark whatever KOReader is doing.
