## Ready-made looks for your shelf

A theme pack is a whole [theme](themes.md) in one folder: framed art and ornaments, a wallpaper, planks and colours, each drawn to sit right on the shelf and to look good on black and white screens as well as colour.

Packs are sold through the Ko-fi shop. Buying one is one way to support Bookshelf's development; you can also [leave a tip](https://ko-fi.com/andyhazz).

## Installing a pack

1. Download the pack's zip from Ko-fi and unzip it on your computer.
2. Connect your e-reader and copy the pack's folder (`Ukiyo-e`, say) into `koreader/settings/bookshelf/ornaments/` on the device. On a Kobo that's inside `.adds/koreader`.
3. Eject, and open your shelf. The pack is a new card in the **Theme library**.
4. Choose it for one shelf, or make it your **Default theme**.

!shot pack-theme-library-ukiyo-e | The Ukiyo-e card in the Theme library.

Packs need Bookshelf 5.3 or later. **Add theme pack…** at the bottom of the Theme library shows the exact folder on your device.

!!! note "Ornaments join your Custom theme too"
    Once a pack is installed, its ornaments are switched on in your **Custom theme** as well, so they may turn up on shelves that don't wear the pack. To keep them to the pack's own theme, open **Theme > Ornaments** on a Custom theme shelf, go to the pack's tab and tap **Select none**.

## Make your own

A pack is just a folder. Put ornaments in it, and a `theme` folder with a `wallpaper.png`, planks or `colours.json`, and it becomes a theme with its own card. The [README](https://github.com/AndyHazz/bookshelf.koplugin#spines) has the details for pack makers.
