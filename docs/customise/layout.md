# Layout, fonts and animations

## Columns and rows

!shot:side adjust-size | Adjust shelf/top panel size.

**Bookshelf > Adjust shelf/top panel size…** sets how many covers fit across (**Columns**, 2 to 6) and how many rows of them the shelf gets (**Rows**). The top panel fills the rest. It works in portrait and landscape alike. Pinching the shelf does the same, quicker.

List and spine shelves have their own row counts, in each shelf's **View mode**.

## Text sizes

!shot:side text-size-menu | Settings > Text size.

**Bookshelf > Settings > Text size** scales each part on its own: **Cover captions**, **Cover badges**, **Stack & folder labels**, **Spine text**, **Shelf menu**, **List text**, **Top panel**, **Modules**, **Start menu** and **Modal tabs** (the tabs in a book's menu).

Each opens a slim page at the bottom of the screen, so the shelf behind shows the change as you make it. Its title gives the size (**Shelf menu font scale: 118%**, say).

!shot size-slider | A size page: the slider, its steps, and Default.

 Drag the slider or tap the steps either side of it, then **Done** to keep it, **Default** to go back to 100%, or **Cancel** to leave it as it was. A tap outside the page keeps it too. Swipe left or right on the shelf behind to see the change on other pages.

## Fonts

Bookshelf comes with three fonts: **Roboto Condensed** for its interface, **Inter ExtraBold** for the top panel's title and **Caveat** for the handwritten author line.

!shot:side ui-font-picker | Bookshelf UI font.

- **Bookshelf > Settings > Bookshelf UI font** sets the interface font, or **Follow KOReader**.
- Each top panel and list line has its own font, in its [line editor](top-panel.md#edit-a-line).

## Image contrast

**Bookshelf > Settings > Image contrast** gives pictures more punch on a screen with few greys:

- **Covers and ornaments contrast boost** deepens the darks and lifts the lights of covers, spines and ornaments. Each picture keeps its own middle grey, so a dark ornament stays dark and a pale cover stays pale.
- **Wallpaper contrast** and **Wallpaper brightness** adjust the wallpaper on its own.

All three start at 0%, the pictures as they are. Each opens a slider page like the text sizes; swipe left or right on the shelf behind it to see the change on other pages before you tap **Done**.

## Animations

On e-ink, Bookshelf can wipe across the screen as it changes, so a change reads as movement rather than a flash. Under **Bookshelf > Settings > Behavior**:

- **Page turn animation**: shelf page turns, and paging the shelf menu.
- **Start menu animation**: the start menu opening and closing.

Each can be **Off**, **Fast** (the default), **Normal** or **Slow**. They only show on e-ink screens.

## Spine text

**Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Spine text direction**: **Top to bottom**, as British and American books are printed, or **Bottom to top**, as in much of Europe.
