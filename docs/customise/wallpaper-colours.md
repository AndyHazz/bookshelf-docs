# Wallpaper and colours

Both live in the Theme menu: open KOReader's menu, then **Theme** on the Bookshelf tab. Like everything there, they're part of the [theme](themes.md) on screen.

## Wallpaper

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

**Theme > Wallpaper** holds:

**Wallpaper**
:   A picture behind the whole shelf. The browser shows each one large, with tabs for **All**, **Yours** and each pack. **No wallpaper** turns it off. One comes with Bookshelf: **Leafy wallpaper**.

**Full screen wallpaper**
:   A different picture for full screen shelves, or **Same as wallpaper**. Full screen is wall-to-wall books, where a busy picture can be too much.

**Invert wallpaper when dark**
:   Show the picture as its negative on a dark shelf.

**Color behind wallpaper**
:   The page colour, with or without a picture.

**Panel colors and shading**
:   How strongly the top panel and footer are shaded over the picture, so they stay readable: **Transparent**, **Low**, **Moderate**, **Heavy** or **Solid**. Below them, **Panel color** gives the panels a colour of their own in place of white or black, and **Panel text** the colour of what's on them. Leave **Panel text** alone and it turns black or white to suit the panel.

**Blur wallpaper behind panels**
:   Frosted glass behind the panels.

**Panel behind Covers shelves**
:   One panel behind everything on a Covers shelf, instead of a strip under each title.

### Your own pictures

Copy PNG or JPEG pictures into `koreader/settings/bookshelf/wallpapers` on your device and they appear under **Yours**. A portrait picture the size of your screen looks best.

If you keep pictures somewhere else already, **Extra wallpaper folder** at the bottom of the menu adds that folder too, without changing anything in it.

## Colours

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip colours | Theme > Colors: a new progress bar and selected tab.

<div markdown>

**Theme > Colors** has every colour on the shelf. Tap a row to pick: a palette and a hex box on a colour screen, or a "% black" nudge on black and white. **Long-press** a row to reset just that colour.

Bookshelf keeps **separate colours for a light shelf and a dark shelf**. **Colors for: Light** (or **Dark**) at the top shows which set you're editing. Tap it to switch.

**Reset to default colors** puts the whole palette back.

??? note "Every colour"
    | Colour | Is |
    |--------|----|
    | **Text ink** | the shelf's own text |
    | **Progress bar**, **Progress bar track** | the bar on covers you're reading |
    | **Bookmark color**, **Finished bookmark color** | the marks on books you're reading and have finished |
    | **Favorite heart color** (or star) | the favourite mark |
    | **Badge foreground**, **Badge background** | the "#3" and "320 p" pills |
    | **Micro-module background**, **Micro-module border** | the cards behind micro-modules |
    | **Border color** | cover frames, badge edges, folder card edges |
    | **Selection outline color** | the ring round the selected book |
    | **Cover shadow color** | the shadow behind covers |
    | **Folder overlay background**, **Folder text color** | the cardboard on folder tiles, and its label |
    | **Shelf menu background** | the bar behind the shelf tabs, or **Transparent** |
    | **Selected shelf background**, **Selected shelf text** | the tab you're on |

On colour e-ink, **Bookshelf > Settings > Advanced > Performance tweaks > Color panel dithering** keeps covers vivid when the shelf redraws. It's on by default.
