# Series, authors and genres

Grouped shelves show one stack per series, author, genre, collection, format, rating or language. Tap a stack to open it. These come from each book's own metadata, or from Calibre's if you use [Calibre metadata](other-libraries.md#calibre).

A book with several authors, genres or collections appears under each of them. To show it only once, turn on **Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Show each book in one group only**: it stays under its first author or genre, or the collection highest in KOReader's collection list. Search and filters still find it under all of them.

## Series

The **Series** shelf shows each series as a **Collage** of its covers, most recently read first. Open a series and its books are in order.

Elsewhere, the **#3** badge on a cover says where a book sits in its series. Choose when it shows under **Bookshelf > Settings > Cover display > Show series #**: **Always**, **Within series folder** or **Never**.

### Series boxes

On a spines shelf, turn on **Shelf style > Series boxes** and each series stands as an **open slipcase** holding its books, with the first cover on the front as a sticker and a count of how many you've read, like "2 / 8". Tap a box to open the series, its books face out in order.

A series with only one book on the shelf stays an ordinary book.

## Authors

Add an **Authors** shelf for a stack per author, or **Specific author…** for one author's books.

!shot:wide authors-shelf | An Authors shelf

**The same author two ways?** Some books store "Osman, Richard", others "Richard Osman". **Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Author name formatting** sets **First Last** or **Last, First** for every book, and merges the variants either way. Surname sorting understands particles, so *de Maupassant* sorts under M.

### Author photos

**Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Find author images online…** looks up a photo for each author without one, from Open Library, or Wikipedia when Open Library has none. Progress shows in the top panel's status line, with **Stop**. Nothing is downloaded until you ask. Each photo is cropped to a book cover's shape, so author stacks line up with the covers around them.

!shot:side author-menu | Long-press an author's stack

For one author, long-press their stack and choose **Find author image online**. A wrong person? Long-press again for **Clear author image**.

## Genres

!shot:wide genres-shelf | The Genres shelf, as ribbons

The **Genres** shelf shows the biggest genres first. Genres come from the book's own tags (or Calibre's tags, or [Hardcover](hardcover.md)'s genres for linked books).

To change a book's genres, long-press it, open the **Tags** tab and tap **Edit** in the Genres section. You can add a new tag there too. Long-press a genre pill to **Rename** it, or **Pin to shelf menu** to make it a shelf. These edits are for that one book, and aren't sent back to Calibre.

**Hide single-book series and genres**, under **Settings > Library & search**, tidies grouped shelves in a library where lots of books have a genre of their own.

## Pictures for stacks

Any series, author, genre or collection stack can have a picture of your own. Long-press it and choose **Set … image…**, or drop images into the **image library**: see [Folders](folders.md#pictures-for-folders-and-stacks).

## How stacks look

Stacks follow the shelf's **Folder tiles** style on a covers shelf (**Shelf style > Folder tiles**): **Divider card**, **Ribbon**, **Book stack**, **Collage**, **Text** or **None**. A count badge on each stack says how many books it holds; change it under **Settings > Cover display > Stack count badge**.
