# Collections and tags

Bookshelf uses **KOReader's own collections**. A collection you make in Bookshelf appears in KOReader's collections menu, and the other way round. **Favorites** is a collection too, always there.

## Put a book in a collection

!shot:side book-tags | The book menu's Tags tab

1. Long-press the book.
2. Open the **Tags** tab and tap **Edit**.
3. Tap **+ Add** under **Collections**.
4. Tick the collections, and tap **Save**.

While editing, the cross on a collection takes the book out of it. Tap **Done** to finish.

!shot collections-edit | Edit collections: tick, then Save

For lots of books at once, [select them](../use/book-menu.md#many-books-at-once) and use the **Collections** row in **Edit selected**: tap a collection once to add the books, again to remove them, and again to leave it as it is, then **Apply**.

## Manage collections

!shot:side manage-collections | Manage collections

**Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Manage collections…** lists them all:

- **+ New collection** makes one.
- Tap a collection for **Rename**, **Pin to shelf menu** and **Delete**.

## A shelf you arrange yourself

A collection can be a shelf whose books stand in the order you put them: a to-be-read pile, a reading challenge, the books you'd grab in a fire. It's still an ordinary KOReader collection underneath.

!clip collection-shelf | Make a shelf from a collection and arrange it

### Make one

From a book
:   Long-press it and tap **Add to a shelf** on the Edit tab. The **Add to shelf** window lists your shelves of this kind: tap one to put the book on it (tap a lit one to take it off). **New shelf…** asks for a name and makes a shelf right after the one you're on, with the book on it. For several books at once, [select them](../use/book-menu.md#many-books-at-once) and use **Add to shelf** under **Right away**.

From the Source page
:   In the shelf editor, tap **Source**, then **Your own collection**, under the Home cards. Tap a collection to use it, or **+ New collection** to make one.

A new shelf made from the Source page starts as Spines; one made from a book looks like the shelf you made it from. On spines every book faces out until you turn it (**Face out: Choose for each book**), and no ornaments are dealt, so you can place your own. Change any of that in its **View mode**.

### Arrange the books

**Long-press a book** on the shelf. Instead of the book menu, **Arrange: *title*** opens and the book is lifted. The menu stays open while you work:

- **Start**, **Page**, **Left**, **Right**, **Page** and **End** move it to the very start of the shelf, first on the page before, one place left or right, first on the next page, or the very end.
- **Swipe the book** left or right to move it one place.
- **Tap another book** to pick that one up instead.
- **Long-press another book or ornament** to swap their places.
- **Swipe the shelf** anywhere else to turn the page with the book still picked up, to take it further.
- **Face out** (on a spines shelf set to **Choose for each book**) turns this book cover forward or spine out.
- **Remove from collection** takes it off the shelf straight away, and says so.
- **Add ornament…** (spines, with **Settings > Modules > On the shelf** on) picks an ornament from any pack and stands it next to the book. **Tap an ornament** to arrange it the same way; see [Pin one in place](../customise/ornaments.md#pin-one-in-place).

The strip at the top names the collection, with **Edit shelf** for the shelf editor and **Edit** for the book's own menu. A tap anywhere else closes it.

Filters don't stop you arranging a shelf. Two things do, and then a long-press opens the book menu as usual:

- **Sorted another way**: the shelf's first sort isn't **Collection order**. Set **Sort** to **Collection order** in the shelf editor.
- **Series boxes** on: each series stands as one box, so its books can't be placed one by one. Turn them off in the shelf editor.

A collection KOReader sorts itself (by title, say) isn't in an order of your own yet. Arrange opens and says so: **Place by hand** starts from the order on screen and turns KOReader's own sorting of that collection off.

## Collections as shelves

- **Pin** one: **Manage collections… > Pin to shelf menu**, or long-press a collection's stack and choose **Pin to the shelf menu**. It can be arranged as above.
- A **Collections** shelf shows every collection as a stack.
- Any shelf can be **filtered** by collection, keeping books in some collections and leaving out books in others. See [Filters](sources-filters.md#genre-and-collection).
- Spines can stand a collection's books **face out** on any shelf: **View mode > Face out > In a collection**.

!!! tip "A To read pile that stands out"
    Make a collection called To read. On your Home shelf, set **Face out** to **In a collection** and choose To read. Your pile stands cover-forward among the spines.

## Tags and genres

A book's genres are its tags. Change them in the **Tags** tab: tap **Edit**, then the cross on a genre to remove it, or **+ Add** under **Genres** to tick more or add a **New tag**. Long-press a genre pill to **Rename** or **Delete** it for that book, or **Pin to shelf menu** to make it a shelf.

Genre edits made here belong to that one book on this device. They don't change the book file, and they aren't sent back to Calibre: if you manage tags in Calibre, edit them there.
