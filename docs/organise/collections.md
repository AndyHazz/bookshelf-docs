# Collections and tags

Bookshelf uses **KOReader's own collections**. A collection you make in Bookshelf appears in KOReader's collections menu, and the other way round. **Favorites** is a collection too, always there.

## Put a book in a collection

!shot:side book-tags | The book menu's Tags tab

1. Long-press the book.
2. Open the **Tags** tab and tap **Edit**.
3. Tap **+ Add** under **Collections**.
4. Tick the collections, and tap **Save**.

While editing, the cross on a collection takes the book out of it. Tap **Done** to finish.

!shot collections-edit | Edit collections: tick, then Save

For lots of books at once, [select them](../use/book-menu.md#many-books-at-once) and use the **Collections** row in **Edit selected**: tap a collection once to add the books, again to remove them, and again to leave it as it is, then **Apply**.

## Manage collections

!shot:side manage-collections | Manage collections

**Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search > Manage collections…** lists them all:

- **+ New collection** makes one.
- Tap a collection for **Rename**, **Pin to shelf menu** and **Delete**.

## Collections as shelves

- **Pin** one: **Manage collections… > Pin to shelf menu**, or long-press a collection's stack and choose **Pin to the shelf menu**.
- A **Collections** shelf shows every collection as a stack.
- A shelf of one collection (**A collection ›**) can keep the collection's own order: sort by **Collection order**, and rearrange it with **Edit collection order**.
- Any shelf can be **filtered** by collection, keeping books in some collections and leaving out books in others. See [Filters](sources-filters.md#genre-and-collection).
- Spines can stand a collection's books **face out**: **View mode > Face out > In a collection**.

!!! tip "A To read pile that stands out"
    Make a collection called To read. On your Home shelf, set **Face out** to **In a collection** and choose To read. Your pile stands cover-forward among the spines.

## Tags and genres

A book's genres are its tags. Change them in the **Tags** tab: tap **Edit**, then the cross on a genre to remove it, or **+ Add** under **Genres** to tick more or add a **New tag**. Long-press a genre pill to **Rename** or **Delete** it for that book, or **Pin to shelf menu** to make it a shelf.

Genre edits made here belong to that one book on this device. They don't change the book file, and they aren't sent back to Calibre: if you manage tags in Calibre, edit them there.
