# Online catalogues (OPDS)

OPDS is how online libraries list their books. Bookshelf can show a catalogue as a shelf: Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, the Internet Archive, or your own Calibre, Komga or Kavita server.

## Add a catalogue shelf

1. Long-press a shelf's tab, tap the **plus** icon, and choose **OPDS catalog…** as the source.
2. Pick a catalogue from the list, or **Add catalog…** with a name, URL and, if it needs one, a username and password.

The list is shared with KOReader's own OPDS plugin, so catalogues you added there are already here.

!!! warning "Set a home folder first"
    Downloads go into your library, so they need a [home folder](../get-started/home-folder.md) that you've set yourself. Without one, a download stops with a message asking you to set it.

## Browsing

A catalogue looks like any other shelf. Categories are tiles: tap to go in, swipe back to come out. Covers load in the background as you page, a few at a time.

The page counter reads "page 3 of 12+" while the catalogue is still loading more. Tap the **last page** arrow to walk to the real end: it counts as it goes, and a tap stops it.

Everything you've seen is kept on the device, so a catalogue you've browsed opens instantly and works offline. Swipe down to fetch it fresh.

## Downloading

**Tap a book** to preview it in the top panel. **Tap it again** for the download view: the best format for KOReader comes first, and when the download finishes, **Read** opens it (or **Not now** leaves it on the shelf).

## Per-catalogue settings

Long-press the shelf's tab and open **Catalog settings**:

- **Saves to**: a folder for this catalogue's downloads, inside your home folder.
- **Refresh**: **Only when I swipe down** (the default), after five minutes, an hour, a day or a week, or **Every time I open it**.
- **Starts at**: open the shelf at a category rather than the top. Set it by long-pressing a category tile and choosing **Start this shelf at**.

## Searching

On a catalogue shelf, the magnifier offers **Search *catalogue name*** to search the catalogue itself, or **Search my library** to check whether you already have a book.

## Comics and manga

Servers that stream comics page by page (Komga, Kavita, Suwayomi) get KOReader's **Page stream** buttons in the download view, to read without downloading.

Catalogues that only lend DRM-protected books can't be used, because KOReader doesn't support DRM.
