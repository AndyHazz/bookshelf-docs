## Shelves that fit how you read

Every shelf is built from your books' own details: series, authors, genres, languages, collections, reading status, ratings, or Calibre's metadata if you use it. Mix a source, a few filters and a sort, and a shelf keeps itself up to date as you add books and read them.

## What's in the box

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Shelves for everything**

    Home, Recent, Series and Genres out of the box. Add your own from any series, author, genre, collection, folder, reading status or online catalogue.

-   **A top panel that's yours**

    The book you are reading, with its blurb, progress and time left. Every line is editable, down to the font.

-   **Spines with real proportions**

    Width from the page count, height from the cover, colour from the cover art.

-   **A start menu**

    A launcher you build yourself: shortcuts, folders, other plugins and live micro-modules like a clock or your reading goal.

-   **Online catalogues**

    Browse Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks and other OPDS catalogues as shelves, and download straight to your device.

-   **Made for e-ink**

    Built and tested on a real e-reader: quick page turns, careful screen refreshes, black and white or colour.

</div>
