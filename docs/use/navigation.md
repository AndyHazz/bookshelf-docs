# Moving around

## Pages

- **Swipe left or right** on the shelf to turn the page. The footer's arrows do the same.
- **Long-press an arrow** to skip ten pages.
- The double arrows jump to the **first** and **last** page.

On e-ink, a page turn plays as a quick wipe across the screen. Its speed is under **Bookshelf > Settings > Behavior > Page turn animation**: **Off**, **Fast**, **Normal** or **Slow**.

## Go to a page, a letter or a book

!shot:side goto-dialog | Tap the page counter

**Tap the page counter.** A small dialog asks for:

- a **page number** on a covers or list shelf, or a **book number** on a spines shelf (spine shelves fit a different number of books per row, so the counter reads as a range like "9-16 of 247");
- a **letter**, with **Go to letter**, to jump to that point in the shelf;
- any **text**, with **Search…**, to search the library.

## Shelves

Tap a tab in the shelf menu to switch shelves. If you have more shelves than fit, **swipe the shelf menu** sideways, or tap the arrows at its ends.

Swiping past the last page of a shelf wraps round to its first page (and past the first, to the last). To step through shelves with a gesture, set **Bookshelf: next shelf** and **Bookshelf: previous shelf** in KOReader's Gesture manager.

## Full screen shelves

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip pinch-rows | Pinch, spread, then full screen.

<div markdown>

- **Swipe up** anywhere on the shelf: the top panel folds away to a thin status line and more books fit.
- **Swipe down**, or tap the status line, to bring it back.

!shot fullscreen-strip | Full screen: the status line above the shelf menu

The shelf remembers, even after a restart.

On the top panel itself, **swipe down** for a bigger panel and one shelf row fewer, and **swipe up** to give the row back.

### Bigger, smaller, more, fewer

- **Pinch** the shelf for more, smaller books.
- **Spread** for fewer, bigger ones.

On covers this changes the columns. On lists and spines it changes the rows, for that shelf only.

## The book you're reading

The **book icon** at the very start of the shelf menu puts the book you're reading back in the top panel, and leaves full screen. Tap the top panel's cover to carry on reading.

**Swipe left or right on the top panel** to preview the next or previous book on the shelf, without leaving your place.

## Refreshing

Bookshelf picks up new books on its own while it's open. To check straight away, **swipe down** on the shelf.

## With buttons

On devices with page-turn buttons, they turn shelf pages. On D-pad devices the arrow keys move a cursor between the shelf menu, the top panel, the books and the footer, and **Press** acts on whatever is selected. See [Gestures](../reference/gestures.md) for the keys.
