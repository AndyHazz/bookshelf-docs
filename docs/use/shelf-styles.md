# Covers, lists and spines

Every shelf can be drawn three ways, and each shelf remembers its own.

<div class="clips" markdown>

!still style-covers | **Covers**: a grid of cover art, with a caption under each.

!still style-list | **List**: titles, authors and progress, row by row.

!still style-spines | **Spines**: books edge-on on a wooden plank.

</div>

## The quick way

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

**Long-press the page counter** in the footer. Each long-press moves the shelf on screen to the next style: Covers, then List, then Spines, then back to Covers.

It changes only the shelf you're looking at. Home can be a grid of covers while Recent is a list.

!!! note "A fresh install starts on spines"
    On a new install the Home shelf shows spines, with your favourites, the books you're reading and the five newest standing face out, so it can look like covers at first. If you'd rather a grid of covers, long-press the page counter once. The videos on this site mostly show Home as covers.

</div>

!clip shelf-styles | Long-press the page counter to step through the styles.

</div>

## The full set of options

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip shelf-editor-style | Long-press a shelf's tab, then View mode.

<div markdown>

**Long-press a shelf's tab** to open its editor, then tap **View mode**. The page has:

- **Show as**: **Auto**, **Covers**, **List** or **Spines**. An online catalogue has no Spines.
- Under it, the settings for the style you picked (below). They change with it.

Everything applies as you tap, and the shelf redraws behind the page. Go back with the arrow at the top left, or close the editor with the X.

The editor's card names the style: **View mode: List**, say, or **View mode: Spines, face out** when some books turn to show their covers. The shelf's [theme](../customise/themes.md) is a card of its own on the editor's front page, **Theme**.

</div>

</div>

## Covers

The grid most people settle on. Each cover can carry small badges: a heart for favourites, the number in its series, a bookmark while you're reading, a mark once finished, and a progress bar. Choose which under **Bookshelf > Settings > Cover display** (see [Layout](../customise/layout.md#on-every-cover)).

<img class="shelfrow" src="../../assets/ui/cover-row-2-light.webp#only-light" alt="Covers with series numbers, favourite hearts and finished marks" style="max-width:620px;margin:1.2rem auto 1.6rem"><img class="shelfrow" src="../../assets/ui/cover-row-2-dark.webp#only-dark" alt="Covers with series numbers, favourite hearts and finished marks" style="max-width:620px;margin:1.2rem auto 1.6rem">

**How big.** The number of columns sets the size: **pinch** the shelf for more, smaller covers, **spread** for fewer, bigger ones. Or use **Bookshelf > Adjust shelf/top panel size…** for exact numbers.

!shot:side shelf-style-covers | View mode on a Covers shelf

On the **View mode** page:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Folder tiles** | How folders, series and authors look: **Divider card**, **Ribbon**, **Book stack**, **Collage**, **Text** or **None**. **Default setting** follows the library-wide choice in **Bookshelf > Settings > Cover display > Default folder style**. |
| **Cover captions** | The line under each cover: **Default** (what **Cover display** says), **None**, **Title**, **Author** or **Series**, or **A line of your own**, built from [tokens](../reference/tokens.md). |
| **Series boxes** | **On** gives each series one tile, on a shelf of books and inside an author or other group. List view has it too. |

!clip folder-style | Default folder style: from Divider card to Ribbon.

## List

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip list-view | The Recent shelf as a list, and its options.

<div markdown>

!shot style-row-list | The Recent shelf as a list

!shot shelf-style-list | View mode on a List shelf

Each row shows a cover thumbnail beside up to six lines of text: by default the title with rating and favourite, the author, the start of the blurb, and a progress bar with pages.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Columns** | 1 to 3 columns side by side, or Auto. |
| **Rows** | 1 to 12 rows a page, or Auto to fit the screen. |

Each has **−** and **+**. Below 1 is Auto.

Pinching a list fits one more or one fewer row. To change what the lines say, see **Bookshelf > Settings > List view**, which works like the [top panel](../customise/top-panel.md) line editor. All list shelves share one layout.

## Spines

Books stand edge-on on a plank, like a real bookcase, so lots fit at once. Some people love it; if it's not for you, the other two styles do everything it does. Each book is drawn from what Bookshelf knows about it:

- **Width** from the page count, so a doorstop is fatter than a novella. Until a book has a page count it gets an average width: see [Page counts](../organise/page-counts.md).
- **Height** from the cover's shape.
- **Colour** from the cover art, darkened so the title stays readable.

<img class="shelfrow" src="../../assets/ui/shelf-row-1-light.webp#only-light" alt="A row of book spines on an oak plank, with a potted cactus at the end"><img class="shelfrow" src="../../assets/ui/shelf-row-1-dark.webp#only-dark" alt="A row of book spines on an oak plank, with a potted cactus at the end">

Tap a spine and the book lifts off the shelf into the top panel. Tap it again to open it, or tap empty space to put it back.

!shot:side shelf-style-spines | View mode on a Spines shelf

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Face out** | Which books turn to show their cover. See below. |
| **Rows** | How many shelves fit on the screen. **Auto** fits the space. |
| **Thickness** | Fatter or thinner spines across the board, 60% to 200% (100% to begin with). |
| **Book sizes** | **Varied** takes each book's height from its cover and thickness from its page count. **Equal height** stands every book at one height, keeping its own width and thickness, so a landscape book facing out shows its whole cover. **Same** stands every book at one size. |
| **Book binding** | **Hardback** or **Paperback**: thin flush covers, softer shading and lighter page edges. A series can have its own from its long-press menu. |
| **Series boxes** | Stands each series as an open slipcase holding its books, with a read count: **Cover** puts the first cover on the front, **Plain** just the series name, **Spines** the books spine-out with the first cover across them. Tap one to open the series. |
| **Author on spine** | **On** prints the author under the title. |
| **Ornaments** | How often [ornaments](../customise/ornaments.md) stand in the gaps: **None**, **Rarely**, **Often** or **Always**. |

The plank and ornaments come from the shelf's [theme](../customise/themes.md), and [theme packs](../customise/packs.md) bring more.

### Face out

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

Like a bookshop dressing a shelf, some books can turn to show their cover. **Face out** opens a page where you check as many reasons as you like:

- **Reading status**: **Unread**, **Reading**, **On hold**, **Finished**
- **Series**: **First in series**, **First unread in series**, or **Unread standalone** (unread books that aren't in a series)
- **Other**: **Favorites**, **Recently added** with **How many of the newest** (1, 3, 5, 10 or 20), and **In a collection**, then choose which one: a To read pile, say
- **Whole shelf**: **All books**, or **None**

The line at the top says what's chosen. The Home shelf ships with Favorites, Reading and the five newest. A new shelf starts with Favorites.

## Auto: a style per level

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip auto-levels | Genres as a list, each genre opening as its own shelf.

<div markdown>

**Auto** picks a style for each level of a shelf:

- **Top level** is the shelf itself.
- **Inside folders and stacks** is anything you open from it: a series, an author, a folder.

!shot shelf-style-auto | View mode on an Auto shelf

Pick **Covers**, **List** or **Spines** for each. So a Genres shelf can **list** its genres and open each genre as a **grid of covers**. Under **Settings**, a card for each level (**Top level: List**, say) opens that style's settings for it. When both levels are lists, each gets its own rows and columns.

A shelf of shelves has no levels to open, so it isn't offered Auto.

</div>

</div>

## Search results

Search results have their own style, separate from any shelf. **Long-press the Search results pill** in the shelf menu for the **Search results** page, with its own **Show as**: **Auto**, **Covers**, **List** or **Spines**. Under Auto it has two levels, **Results** and **Inside folders and stacks**, the same way a shelf does. A series or author you open from the results keeps search's style. See [Search](search.md).
