# Search

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

Tap the **magnifier** at the end of the shelf menu, type, and tap **Search**.

!shot search-dialog | Search library

Search looks across **titles, authors, series, genres and file names** at once and groups the results. Tap a series or author in the results to open it.

The shelf menu shows **Back**, then **Search results**, then what you searched for:

!shot search-results-crumb | After searching for "verne"

- **Back** returns you to where you were.
- **Tap** your search to change it.
- **Long-press Search results** for its own page, with **Show as**: **Auto**, **Covers**, **List** or **Spines**. Under **Auto** you pick a style for **Results** and another for **Inside folders and stacks**, what you open from them. Results start as covers; under Auto, both levels start as lists.
- **Long-press the page counter** on the results to switch between covers and a list.

A series or author you open from the results keeps search's style rather than its own shelf's.

## Fine-tuning

Two switches under **Bookshelf > Settings > Library & search**:

**Include folder names in search results**
:   Off by default. Folders usually mirror your authors or series, so matching them just repeats results. Turn it on if you find books by folder.

**Include genres and tags in search results**
:   On by default. If a common tag crowds out the book you meant, turn it off and search matches only titles, authors and series.

## Other ways to find a book

- **Tap the page counter** and type a letter to jump to it on the shelf.
- On an [online catalogue](../organise/opds.md) shelf, the magnifier searches the catalogue itself, with **Search my library** alongside.
