# Your first five minutes

<p class="lead">Here's your shelf, with Home switched to a grid of covers. What you're looking at, and the few gestures that get you everywhere.</p>

<div class="walk" markdown>

<div markdown>

## The top panel { .minor }

!shot top-panel | The top panel

The big card at the top shows the book you're reading: its cover, title, author, blurb, a progress bar and the time left. **Tap the cover** to carry on reading. Tap the blurb to read all of it.

## The shelf menu { .minor }

!shot shelf-menu-row | The shelf menu

The row of tabs below it is the **shelf menu**. Each tab is a shelf, a different view of the same library:

- **Home**: all your books. A fresh install shows them as spines on a wooden shelf: long-press the page counter once for a grid of covers, as in the videos here.
- **Recent**: what you've opened lately, as a list.
- **Series**: one stack per series.
- **Genres**: one stack per genre.

The book icon at the start of the row brings the book you're reading back to the top panel. The magnifier at the end is [search](../use/search.md).

## The shelf { .minor }

Your books. **Tap a book** and it shows in the top panel. **Tap it again** to open it.

## The footer { .minor }

!shot shelf-footer | The footer

Arrows turn pages, and the counter in the middle says where you are. The menu button on the left is your [start menu](../use/start-menu.md), a launcher you can fill with shortcuts.

## Five gestures worth knowing

| Do this | Where | And |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Swipe left or right** | the shelf | turn the page |
| **Long-press** | a book | its book menu: favourite, finished, collections, cover |
| **Long-press** | a shelf tab | edit that shelf: its style, source, filters and sort |
| **Long-press** | the page counter | switch between spines, covers and a list |
| **Swipe up**, then **down** | the shelf | full screen shelves, then the top panel back |

There's a full list in [Gestures](../reference/gestures.md).

## Two settings to try first

**Covers or spines?** Home starts as spines. **Long-press the page counter** in the footer to switch it to a grid of covers, then a list, then back. See [Covers, lists and spines](../use/shelf-styles.md).

<div class="shots" markdown>

!shot style-row-covers | Covers

**Folder tiles.** Shelves show folders as **Divider card** tiles in Covers view: a cardboard tab with the folder's name on it. If your covers have a title band of their own, that can look busy. **Ribbon** puts the name on a band across the first book's cover instead, which often looks cleaner.

<div class="walk flip" markdown>

!clip folder-style | Changing the folder style from Divider card to Ribbon.

<div markdown>

1. Tap the top of the screen to open KOReader's menu, and pick the **Bookshelf** tab (the open-book icon, second from the left).
2. Go to **Settings > Cover display > Default folder style**.
3. Pick **Ribbon**. The shelf behind the menu changes as you tap, so try a few.

Each shelf can choose its own folder style too: long-press its tab, open **Shelf style** and tap **Folder tiles**. This is covered in [Covers, lists and spines](../use/shelf-styles.md).
